Projects

I love letting my imagination run wild. The feeling that I get when I finish a project is hard to explain. It is so rewarding to actually make something. Being able to make an idea come to life with no instructions and only what you know feels so amazing. Most of my projects started from a cool video or just from some interesting ideas. I hope to one day make a medical machine that can help people.

Mechanically Passive Haptic Feedback System

I was super interested in biomedical engineering, and wanted to learn more, so I decided to do a project on prosthetics. I designed and tested ways to bring the sense of touch back to people who can't afford expensive hand prosthetics. This device is integratable into an open source 3D printed prosthetic model (e-NABLE phoenix v3 hand). This device is also 3D printed, and while it's not entirely 3D printable, it is still very cost effective compared to the other devices on the market.

Sock Donner

A few months ago, my grandfather reached out to me because he was having difficulty putting on his socks. The sock aids available on the market were often one-size-fits-all and didn't accommodate his needs. I decided to design something better. I developed a device that starts compact, making it easy to put a sock on, and expands to accommodate different foot sizes. My goal was to create a more adaptable and accessible solution that makes putting on socks easier.

CAD & 3D Printing Medical Device Innovation Product Development

Tic Tac Watch

I love eating Tic Tacs. I would always have a pack in my soccer bag. I designed this so I will always have Tic Tacs on me when I get hungry. It uses a spring I found to act as an archimedes screw and push a platform to push into the Tic Tacs so they wouldn't shake around and make sound. I also made the watch 2 parts so you can replenish the Tic Tacs easier.

Mechanical Mechanisms Spring Load Assembly Product Design
