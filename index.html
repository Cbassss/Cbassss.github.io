Academics

From Calculus to Chemistry, I try to challenge myself and explore as much as I can. Learning is not just in school for me, I experiment and try new things to get a better understanding of everything I can. Learning new skills opens so many doors, so I try to learn as much as I can. I focus on STEM disciplines, especially math and engineering.

Projects

I love letting my imagination run wild. The feeling that I get when I finish a project is hard to explain. It is so rewarding to actually make something. Being able to make an idea come to life with no instructions and only what you know feels so amazing. Most of my projects started from a cool video or just from some interesting ideas. I hope to one day make a medical machine that can help people.

Employment

Working is a great way to learn people skill, and responsibility. I have been able to learn so much and gain life skills from working and talking with people. Work has also taught me the value of money, and how to save and grow it. Lots of these jobs have had situations that brought me out of my comfort zone, but that is exactly what grows people and I am glad to have experienced these.

About Me

As a Delaware student, I value working hard. My goal is simple: to make the world a better place. When I'm not glued to my computer doing CAD or building parts, I like to play soccer, hang out with friends, and play videogames. I love solving problems and finding new creative ways to do things. I am inspired by people like William Osman who create things for the fun of it, and build things to solve their problems.
